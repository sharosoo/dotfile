# Silver Bullet (Bézier Games, Ted Alspach)

Card game, 2-4 players, 4 rounds, lowest total wins. Part of the Silver series (Silver Amulet, Silver Bullet, Silver Dagger, Silver Coin); decks can be mixed by swapping all cards of one number.

## Sources that worked
- Official rulebook PDF text: gamers-hq.de `Silver_Bullet_Rules.pdf` (via `web_extract`); no per-card powers in it.
- Rules mirror: board-game-rules.com/boardgames/silver-bullet (French card names, same rules).

## Rules facts verified in the rulebook
- Deck 52: Hunter 0 x2, numbers 1-12 x4 each, Copycat 13 x2. Average about 6.5.
- Setup: 5 face-down cards per player (village), look at 2, one face-up discard.
- Turn, exactly one: draw from deck (discard it, using its power if icon, or swap into village face-down), take discard top (swap, placed face-up, power not usable), or Call (village 4 or fewer cards; others get one more turn; no powers on the Call turn).
- Multi-swap needs identical values, slide cards forward and reveal; mismatch returns all face-down and adds the drawn card at an end; only when 3 or more were slid and mismatched, add an unseen deck card too. The card drawn this turn cannot join a set until next turn.
- Score = sum of village. Caller with lowest (ties count) scores 0, else sum + 10. Lowest scorer gets the bullet side; takes it only if they called successfully. Bullet on one own card: unviewable, unmovable, unscored, excluded from the 4-card Call count, not on the placing turn's Call.
- Power timing: 0 at scoring; 1-4 while face-up in village; 5-12 on discard right after drawing from deck; 13 at scoring. Village-discarded cards only use power with a face-up Mortician.

## Powers (secondary sources)
Lycan 1 adds 1 when discarding; Priest 2 and Instigator 6 flip face-up cards down; Goth Girl 3 discards to deck bottom; Mortician 4 uses power of the card discarded from village; Cow 5 flips the deck so draws go face-up; Insomniac 7 views all own face-down; Thing 8 shuffles one village's face-down cards; Marksman 9 uses a face-up card's discard power from any village; Count 10 moves 10 deck cards to discard; Troublemaker 11 swaps cards between two players; Gremlin 12 adds a card to another village without replacing one (the bullet can kill it).
