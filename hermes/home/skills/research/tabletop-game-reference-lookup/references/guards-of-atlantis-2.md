## Rules facts verified in play
- Round = 4 turns (play one card face-down, reveal, resolve by initiative). End of round order: minion battle, remove tokens, push lane, retrieve cards, mandatory level up (pity coin if not leveled).
- Fast travel replaces a whole Movement action (needs a Movement-icon card), jumps to adjacent zones, ignores card text, blocked by interfering enemies.
- Minion coins: melee/ranged 2, heavy 4. Heavy is immune while any friendly non-heavy minion remains in the battle zone.
- Hero kill: attacker gets coins = victim's level; allies get coins = victim's lowest card tier; victim's team loses Life counters = lowest card tier. Level 2/3 heroes can only upgrade to Tier II; Tier III needs all non-basic cards at Tier II first.
- Battle zone moves only when one team has no minions after the minion battle: remove a wave counter, next zone toward the LOSING team's throne becomes the battle zone. Whoever wipes the other's last minion wins the push.
- stats-of-atlantis.org is an SPA with no static art in its HTML; use the cockeyedandcurious card images instead.
