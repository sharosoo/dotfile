# Dated case-study notes from one platform/AI business-model analysis

These are historical benchmarks captured during a prior session. Re-fetch before presenting them as current facts.

## Amazon

- Amazon's 2001 Form 10-K reported 25 million customer accounts and an accumulated deficit of about $2.86 billion.
- Amazon Marketplace orders had reached 15% of U.S. orders in Q4 2001; the company was already operating online retail plus Marketplace/third-party seller channels.
- AWS says it launched in spring 2006, beginning with S3 and EC2. The useful pattern is retailer → marketplace → usage-priced infrastructure, not a single late pivot.
- Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000103221002000059/d10k405.htm ; https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/our-origins/

## Uber

- Uber's Q4 2020 MAPCs were 93 million. FY2020 net loss was $6.768 billion and Adjusted EBITDA loss was $2.528 billion.
- In Q4 2020 Delivery gross bookings grew 128% year over year; Uber described a nearly $44 billion annual Delivery bookings run-rate in December 2020.
- Pattern: ride-hailing distribution and logistics capabilities → Delivery, Freight, membership, and local-commerce adjacency. Net loss is not the same as CAC; the release includes stock compensation and other adjustments.
- Source: https://investor.uber.com/news-events/news/press-release-details/2021/Uber-Announces-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2020/

## Spotify

- Q4 2023: 602 million MAU and 236 million subscribers; FY2023 operating loss was €446 million.
- Q4 2024: 675 million MAU, 263 million subscribers, and €477 million operating income; Spotify called it its first full year of profitability.
- Pattern: music subscription/ads → podcasts, audiobooks, creator tools, and broader ad inventory. This is monetization and adjacency expansion, not simply a user-base pivot.
- Sources: https://newsroom.spotify.com/2024-02-06/spotify-reports-fourth-quarter-2023-earnings/ ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1639920/000163992024000004/ck0001639920-20231231.htm ; https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-02-04/spotify-reports-fourth-quarter-2024-earnings/

## Airbnb

- Airbnb's 2020 Form 10-K reported a $4.6 billion GAAP net loss and a $6.0 billion accumulated deficit at year end.
- The 2020 loss included $2.8 billion of non-cash IPO stock compensation and an $827 million warrant mark-to-market adjustment; 2020 Adjusted EBITDA was a $251 million loss.
- Pattern: COVID survival was cost reduction and core-market refocus (nearby/long-term stays), not evidence of a successful post-MAU pivot. Use this as a counterexample to the claim that losses plus users guarantee a later model.
- Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972021000010/airbnb-10k.htm

## Character.AI

- In October 2024, TechCrunch reported more than 20 million monthly users and more than 100 million user-created characters.
- The $9.99 c.ai+ subscription was described as one of the only consistent revenue sources, while management said it was focusing on growth rather than revenue generation.
- After the founders and many pre-training researchers moved to Google under a $2.7 billion licensing arrangement, the remaining company focused on consumer AI, product, engineering, post-training, and tuning rather than AGI/pre-training. No public P&L should be invented.
- Source: https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/02/character-ai-hires-ex-youtube-exec-as-cpo-says-will-raise-money-next-year-with-new-partners/

## Wrtn

- A 2025 OpenAI case study described Wrtn's product suite as reaching 6.5 million MAU and using router architecture with lightweight models for classification/direction and heavier models for more demanding tasks.
- Reporting on the 2025 financial statements described revenue of about KRW 47.1 billion, operating loss of about KRW 58.8 billion, operating expenses of about KRW 106.0 billion, payment fees of about KRW 61.1 billion, advertising expense of about KRW 29.4 billion, and salary expense of about KRW 9.5 billion.
- The payment-fee line should not be treated as pure LLM serving without disclosure of its internal split. The model is better described as free consumer AI plus character/content products and enterprise AX expansion, with monetization being built alongside growth.
- Sources: https://openai.com/index/wrtn/ ; https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2026041716200471244

## Reusable interpretation

- Historical cases do not establish a fixed "pivot after N years" law. They show several routes: marketplace take-rate, subscription/ads, creator economy, local-commerce adjacency, usage-priced infrastructure, B2B/API, or survival restructuring.
- For AI, MAU is a weak cost proxy. Serving cost depends on sessions, tokens, model-routing mix, image/audio/tool calls, and heavy-user concentration. Broad accounting categories such as payment fees are upper bounds, not pure-serving measurements.
