# Purpose-first H1 for 정혁 research artifacts

Companion to `korean-technical-blog-rewriting/references/purpose-first-h1.md`.

Before publishing a Korean 조사/비교 article:

1. Write one purpose sentence (무엇이 나왔고, 무엇과 어디가 다른지).
2. H1 names that subject and thesis. Not an internal mechanism. Not `X가 연 것은 Y가 아니다`.
3. Put `이 글의 목적` in the meta table.
4. Keep teaching hooks (e.g. leftover plugin registration) inside the relevant section.
