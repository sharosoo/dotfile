# Chinese / Mandarin (中文) translationese checklist

Read before rewriting Chinese text. Check every item, name what remains, regenerate
until clean (max 3 passes). Re-compose; don't patch (patching leaves post-editese).

## Table of contents

## 1. Pronoun overuse
Chinese drops subjects freely and rarely uses 它 for inanimate things; English forces them.
- ✗ `它很重要。它会影响用户。`
- ✓ `这很重要，会影响用户。` (or drop entirely)
Kill mechanical 它/他们/这个 standing in for it/they/this.

## 2. `的` over-stacking & long pre-nominal modifiers
English relative clauses crammed in front of the noun with stacked `的` read translated.
Split into separate clauses or move information after the noun.
- ✗ `这是一个能够处理用户的所有的请求的系统。`
- ✓ `这个系统能处理用户的所有请求。`

## 3. Light-verb padding
English "make/conduct/provide + noun" calqued as 进行/作出/给予 + noun, when a single
verb is natural.
- ✗ `对这个问题进行讨论` → ✓ `讨论这个问题`
- ✗ `作出一个决定` → ✓ `决定` / `做决定`
- ✗ `给予帮助` → ✓ `帮助`

## 4. `被` passive overuse
Chinese uses 被 far less than English passive, and 被 often carries an adverse nuance.
Prefer active or topic-comment.
- ✗ `这个功能被系统处理。` → ✓ `这个功能由系统处理。` / `系统会处理这个功能。`

## 5. Calqued connectives & "because…so" doubling
- `关于` (about) — often deletable or restructure.
- `通过` (through) — often → a verb or `用`.
- Don't render both halves of English subordination: 因为…所以…, 虽然…但是… — natural
  Chinese often keeps only one.
- ✗ `因为下雨了，所以我们取消了活动。` → ✓ `下雨了，活动取消了。` (context permitting)

## 6. Measure-word & plural `们` errors
- Wrong/absent measure word (个 as a catch-all is a tell): `三个建议` ok but `一个信息`
  awkward → `一条信息`.
- `们` only attaches to human nouns and not after numbers: ✗ `三个学生们` → ✓ `三个学生`.

## 7. Topic-comment structure lost
Chinese fronts the topic; translationese keeps rigid English SVO.
- ✗ `我把这本书已经读完了的部分忘记了。` (English-shaped)
- ✓ `这本书我已经读完的部分忘了。`

## 8. Over-formal / calqued vocabulary
Literal translation reaches for stiff or wrong-register words. Match the audience; honor
glossary. Idioms map to Chinese idiom, not gloss:
- ✗ `break the ice` → `打破冰` → ✓ `打破僵局` / `活跃气氛`

## Worked example (regenerate, don't patch)

**Translationese draft:**
> 用户能够对他们的账户的设置进行修改。它可以通过安全标签被访问。

**Symptoms:** `他们的` pronoun calque, stacked `的`, light verb `进行修改`, `它` subject,
`通过`, `被` passive, English word order.

**Native rewrite:**
> 账户设置可以在「安全」标签里修改。

Shorter, re-composed, active — not spot-fixed.
